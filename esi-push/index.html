ESI push

ESI push is a small personal command-line tool. Its single maintainer uses it to append rows to Google Sheets spreadsheets that he owns, from his own computer, as part of a personal research workflow.

What it does

  • Signs in once with Google OAuth and asks for one permission: the Google Sheets scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets).
  • Reads the current state of a spreadsheet the user names, then appends new rows to it and reads them back to confirm the write.
  • Runs only on the maintainer's own machine, started by hand. It has no server, no website backend and no other users.

What it does not do

  • It does not access Gmail, Drive files, Calendar, contacts or any other Google service.
  • It does not send spreadsheet data anywhere other than back to Google Sheets.
  • It is not offered to the public and is not a commercial product.
